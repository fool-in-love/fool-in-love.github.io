Tropical Cyclogenesis & Dvorak Technique

Named after pioneer Vernon Dvorak (whom my handle honors), this methodology utilizes visible and infrared satellite imagery to estimate tropical cyclone intensity ($T$-numbers and Current Intensity $CI$). Deep understanding of Central Dense Overcast (CDO) structures, curved band patterns, eyewall replacement cycles (ERC), and rapid intensification (RI) indicators.

Dvorak T-Numbers GOES-19 IR 10.3µm Rapid Intensification
radar

NEXRAD Level II/III Radar Analysis

Interpretation of base reflectivity ($Z$), storm-relative velocity ($V$), and dual-polarization products. Proficient in distinguishing non-meteorological scatterers using Correlation Coefficient ($\rho_{HV}$), Differential Reflectivity ($Z_{DR}$), and detecting tornadic debris signatures (TDS) and mesocyclonic shear velocity couplets.

WSR-88D Dual-Pol GRLevel3 / Gibson TDS & Velocity Couplets

Thermodynamic Soundings & Skew-T

Comprehensive decoding of raw radiosonde balloon launches on Skew-T Log-P diagrams. Calculation of convective parameters including Surface-Based and Most-Unstable CAPE ($J/kg$), Lifted Condensation Level (LCL), Downdraft CAPE (DCAPE), and $0-1km$ / $0-3km$ Storm-Relative Helicity (SRH).
